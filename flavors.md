# Flavors

3D engineering sim can be implemented in a variety of possible flavors.

- Currently existing approximation-based flavor
  - Assumes manual / semi-automated processes 
  - Engineers run sims with approximations, assuming sufficient safety margins
  - Workflow: 
    - digital objects are designed and tested in sim
    - physical objects (are possibly?) produced from digital blueprints
      - > how exactly is it currently done in the industry? what are best practices? what is the amount of manual labor? in what points / processes?
- Future-proof exact flavor
  - Assumes:
    - entirely automated design-manufacturing process
    - exact model with **atomic fidelity**
  - Engineers run sims with exact values,  (with possible lesser safety margins due to increased precision)
  - Workflow:
    - digital objects are designed and tested in sim
    - physical objects are (possibly) (nano-assembled  ?) / (3D-printed ?) / (CNC-machined ?) from digital blueprints
