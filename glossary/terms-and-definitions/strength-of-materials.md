[Strength of materials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strength_of_materials)

---

An introduction to **stress & strain**

Strength of materials, also known as mechanics of materials, is a branch of engineering that deals with the behavior of solid objects when acted upon by loads.

Because it deals with how objects deform under loading, strength of materials is an essential topic for mechanical and civil engineers.
