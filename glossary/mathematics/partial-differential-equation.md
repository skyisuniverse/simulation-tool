A **partial differential equation (PDE)** is **a mathematical equation that relates an unknown multivariable function to its partial derivatives with respect to two or more independent variables** (such as space and time).

[Partial Differential Equations Overview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pvrIagjEk4c)

Unlike ordinary differential equations (ODEs), which involve derivatives with respect to a single variable, PDEs describe multi-dimensional phenomena like heat flow, wave propagation, fluid dynamics, and quantum mechanics.
