# Materials (in the context of Production Methodology)

What we want? An in-house manufacturing of parts & components for assembly of integrated products.

By default we assume existing materials. In perspective, we assume development and usage of new materials for production:

- materials **comparable to metal** in durability yet **easier in handling** & not requiring massive expensive setup (heavy heating screening, etc.), **cheap and simple** enough in handling to be **produced in-house cheaply, affordably in an automated way**.
- What could such materials be?
- How could those be produced? Synthesized?
- Could we (in perspective) reach such a level of technological solvency, that would **enable production of any kind of integrated product** within the sizes of a typical apartment? (e.g. consumer-grade products, maybe even rockets? Satellites?
  - If yes, it would mean we’d have to **miniaturize the size of components**. If we could succeed with creating a design of miniaturized yet functional components & integrated products made of those, we could possibly:
    - **avoid expensive factory setups**
    - **toxic and dangerous materials**
    - **high energy requirements** 
    - and **expensive electric supply setups**.

## In-house apartment/workshop-based manufacturing

- **Materials** 
    - Durable like metal or better
    - Not energy demanding
    - Easy to handle (manipulate, form, shape, etc.)
    - Sustainable for the environment (not toxic or dangerous)

- Means of **materials production**

- **Miniaturized** (yet functional) **integrated product designs**
    - Miniaturized (yet functional) component designs

- (Easily producible) **Manufacturing machines** 
  - (3D printers with new (metal-grade) materials? 
  - CNC-machines? 
  - DNA-synthesizers? 
  - etc. ?)
