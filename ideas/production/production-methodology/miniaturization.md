# Miniaturization

What we want?

## Material-enabled compact production (?)

An in-house manufacturing of parts & components for assembly of integrated products, with a material comparable to metal in durability yet easier in handling & not requiring massive expensive setup (heavy heating screening, etc.), cheap and simple enough in handling to be produced in-house cheaply, affordably in an automated way.

What could such material be?

How could it be produced? (Synthesized) ?

Could we (in perspective) reach such a level of technological solvency, that would enable production of any kind of integrated product within a typical apartment? (e.g. consumer-grade products, maybe even rockets? Satellites?

If yes, it would mean we’d have to miniaturize the size of components. If we could succeed with creating a design of miniaturized yet functional components & integrated products made of those, we could possibly avoid expensive factory setups, toxic and dangerous materials, high energy requirements and expensive electric supply setups. 


In-house apartment-based manufacturing.

- Materials 
    - Durable like metal or better
    - Not energy demanding
    - Easy to handle (manipulate, form, shape, etc.)
    - Sustainable for the environment (not toxic or dangerous)

- Means of materials production

- Miniaturized (yet functional) integrated product designs
    - Miniaturized (yet functional) component designs

- (Easily producible) Manufacturing machines (3D printers with new (metal-grade) materials? CNC-machines? DNA-synthesizers? etc. ?)
