> - **Build and post-processing**: The platform lowers, another layer is applied, and the cycle repeats. Finished **parts** usually undergo stress-relief heat treatment, support removal, powder cleanup, and optional CNC machining or surface finishing.

> ## Common Applications
>
> - **Aerospace and energy**: Lightweight brackets, turbine components, and complex ducts.
>
> - **Medical and dental**: Custom implants, surgical guides, and dental crowns or frameworks.
>
> - **Automotive and tooling**: Functional prototypes, low-volume production parts, and conformal cooling molds.
