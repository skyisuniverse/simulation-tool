> Why are equipment and operating costs high in DMLS?

**DMLS equipment and operating costs are high** because the process requires precision laser optics, sealed inert-gas chambers, expensive atomized metal powders, strict powder-handling safety gear, and extensive post-processing.


## 1. High Equipment & Facility Costs (CapEx)

- **Industrial machine prices**: Entry-level DMLS systems start around **$150,000–$300,000**, while standard single- or multi-laser production machines typically cost **$500,000 to over $1.5 million**.
- **Precision components**: Machines require high-power fiber lasers, ultra-fast optical galvanometers, precision Z-axis stages, heated build plates, and gas-tight recirculation filters.
- **Ancillary equipment**: You cannot just buy the printer—you also need powder sieving stations, industrial vacuum cleaners (explosion-rated), heat-treatment furnaces, Wire EDM machines for part removal, and CNC mills for finishing.
- **Facility upgrades**: Metal powder is reactive and hazardous to inhale, requiring dedicated rooms, fire-suppression systems (Class D), climate control, and heavy-duty electrical power.


## 2. High Operating & Material Costs (OpEx)

- **Expensive metal powders**: DMLS uses spherical, tightly graded metal powders produced via gas or plasma atomization, costing roughly **$50–$150/kg** for stainless steel or aluminum and **$250–$500+/kg** for titanium or nickel superalloys (far more expensive than solid bar stock).
- **Inert gas consumption**: Chambers must be purged and continuously flushed with high-purity **argon** or **nitrogen** throughout multi-day builds, adding steady consumable expense.
- **Slow build speeds**: Fusing metal 30–60 micrometers at a time means builds often run for **12 to 72+ hours**, resulting in low machine throughput and high hourly machine amortization rates.
- **Labor-intensive post-processing**: Removing loose powder from internal channels, stress-relieving parts in a furnace, cutting parts off the build plate, chiseling/machining away supports, and finishing rough surfaces often account for **30% to 60% of the total part cost**.
- **Consumables and maintenance**: Recoater blades, build plates (which wear down each time they are ground flat), gas filters, and laser optics calibration require regular replacement and service contracts.
