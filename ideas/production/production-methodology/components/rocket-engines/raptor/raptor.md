The SpaceX Raptor is a family of **cryogenic, full-flow staged-combustion rocket engines powered by subcooled liquid methane and liquid oxygen (LOX/CH₄)**.

## Key Design & Architecture Features

- **Full-Flow Staged Combustion (FFSC)**: Both the fuel-rich and oxygen-rich turbopumps are driven by dedicated pre-burners, with 100% of the propellants entering the main combustion chamber in a gaseous state. This increases combustion efficiency (around 99%) and boosts operating pressure.
- **Raptor 3 Simplifications**: Raptor 3 internalizes external plumbing, sensors, and secondary thermal shrouds. It eliminates the need for a separate engine heat shield and reduces dead mass by roughly 500 kg per engine.
- **Vacuum Variant (RVac)**: Features an extended nozzle designed for vacuum operation in space, achieving a higher specific impulse of roughly 380 seconds.
- **Vehicle Application**: The Super Heavy booster uses 33 Raptor engines, while the Starship second stage uses a mix of 3 sea-level Raptors and 3 vacuum Raptors.
