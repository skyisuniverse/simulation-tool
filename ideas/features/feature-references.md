- **File formats frenzy**

  FreeCAD allows you to import and export models and many other kinds of data from your models such as analyses results or quantities data to dozens of different [file formats](https://wiki.freecad.org/Category:File_Formats) such as [STEP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO_10303), [IGES](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IGES), [OBJ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wavefront_.obj_file), [STL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/STL_%28file_format%29), [DWG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/.dwg), [DXF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AutoCAD_DXF), [SVG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SVG), [SHP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shapefile), [DAE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COLLADA), [IFC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Industry_Foundation_Classes) or [OFF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OFF_(file_format)), [NASTRAN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nastran), [VRML](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VRML), [OpenSCAD CSG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OpenSCAD) and many more, in addition to FreeCAD's native [FCStd](https://wiki.freecad.org/File_Format_FCStd) file format. Add-on workbenches can also add more file formats.
