# Practical Feasibility

After diving into lots of domain specific information, including open-source tools, there is a bit of overwhelm with the complexity. We have to find a way to turn all this amazing awesomeness into something quick and practical. That would make the entire process (of simulating something) unobtrusive to such an extent, that the user won't have to necessarily dive into all the details, but will have the possibility to do so would there be such a need. 

After all, what are we willing to simulate for? Possibly,  for:

- getting confidence about some producible thing before it is produced
- getting transparency about it in different circumstances and conditions
- getting a high-quality blueprint that allows to affordably produce it in an automated way
  - e.g. CNC-machining,  3D-printing, DNA-nano-assembly, etc. 

We could summarize it: 

## **We simulate in order to get a blueprint for production**.

This blueprint has to be gotten in a **quick** way. But also in **high quality**.

One good example is cadam - the generated model of a [Starship](https://adam.new/cadam/share/7239151b-d454-4552-85ff-a508e266e1af) in one click. The downsides are the limitations for models usage. It would be nice, if we had such ease of the process, that would allow:

- To generate and edit any blueprint for production with AI 
  - (without limitations for AI models usage)
  - with different types of simulation
- To produce any product from a blueprint
  - with ease in an unobtrusive way
