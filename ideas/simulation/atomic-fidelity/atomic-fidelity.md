## in Quantum Computing & Physics

Atomic fidelity in **Quantum Computing & Physics** relates to:

- the precision and accuracy (fidelity) of quantum operations, 
- single-qubit or two-qubit gates, 
- or photon storage efficiency using neutral atom qubits or atomic arrays (such as in Ytterbium-171 systems or ordered atom arrays).

[Optimization of photon storage fidelity in ordered atomic arrays](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.06312)

[High-fidelity gates and mid-circuit erasure conversion in an atomic qubit](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06438-1)
