# Simulation Synchronization

I assume at some point it could be possible to synchronize the simulation with the physical world. (Necessary) Compute could probably be a matter of time (with nano-assembly / neuromorphic (?) / other (?)).

Humanity currently performs engineering simulation with approximation, running sim with FEM / FEA against meshes (of finite elements (triangles / etc.)). However, in real physical world objects are made of atoms and molecules, not of meshes.

It means, that if we want better simulation, we have to introduce objects into the sim as they are in the physical world.

1. Which means we could possibly learn how to create digital representations of the physical objects that are identical. 

This could be the first step.

2. We could then possibly keep the sim in sync with the real physical world continuously. We'd need tools for continuous physical world scanning, monitoring and continuous world-sim sync-up.

3. If we have perfect digital representations of the physical objects in sim, it means we can run much better (more precise) simulations. (at least much closer to the real world). In short - meshes could be made of not finite elements but digital atoms and molecules. And sim could be run against it. Then it could be much more precise.
