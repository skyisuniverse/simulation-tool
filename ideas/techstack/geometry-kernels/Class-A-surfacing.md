# Class-A surfacing

From conversation with Gemini on [Class-A surfacing](https://share.google/aimode/mA4qc1EDkmsIgdjba)

Class-A surfacing is **the highest standard of aesthetic 3D digital surface modeling**, defining the smooth, visible exterior and interior shell of a product. [Class A surface (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Class_A_surface)

## Key Characteristics

- **Geometric Continuity**: Built strictly with G2 (curvature continuity) or G3 (rate of curvature continuity) so that light glides across the form with zero fractured highlights or visual kinks.

- **Visual Judgment**: Evaluated using diagnostic tools like zebra stripe plots and curvature combs rather than raw dimensional specs alone.

- **Form Follows Function**: Bridges raw artistic intent (from clay models or 3D scans) with rigorous engineering, manufacturing, and tooling requirements.

## Common Applications

- **Automotive**: Visible body panels, fender crowns, hood lines, and interior instrument panels.

- **Consumer Electronics & Products**: Seamless casings for premium devices (such as smartphones and appliances) where touch and light reflections dictate perceived quality.
